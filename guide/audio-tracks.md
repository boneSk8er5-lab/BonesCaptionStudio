# Audio tracks: caption only your mic

If your recording has more than one audio track, a track box appears in **Transcription**. Pick the track with your voice, and the game sound won't get captioned.

## Set up OBS to record your mic on its own track

1. In OBS, open **Settings**, then **Output**, and set **Output Mode** to **Advanced**.
2. On the **Recording** tab, tick more than one **Audio Track** (for example 1 and 2).
3. Close Settings. In the **Audio Mixer**, click the gear on any source and choose **Advanced Audio Properties**.
4. Put your **mic** on track 2 only, and game and desktop audio on track 1.

Your recordings now have the game on one track and your voice on the other.

> The video you export still has all its sound. The track you pick is only used to find the words.
