# Fix the transcript

The **Transcript Editor** under the preview lists every caption line. Click the arrow on a line to see its words.

![The Transcript Editor with a hidden line and voices](transcript-editor.png)

## Fix a wrong word

1. Double-click the line, or a single word under it.
2. Type the right text and click **OK**. Leave a word empty to delete it.

Clicking a line also jumps the preview to that moment.

## Hide lines or words

Select lines or words and click **Hide / Show** (or press `Delete`). Hidden ones are crossed out and won't appear in the captions or exports. Do the same again to bring them back.

To remove words for good, press `Shift+Delete` (or right-click and choose **Delete permanently**).

## Find something

Type in **Search transcript** (`Ctrl+F`) to show only the lines with that word.

## Undo

Made a mistake? Click **↶** (`Ctrl+Z`). **↷** (`Ctrl+Y`) does it again.

## Right-click menu

Right-click a line for **Hide / Show**, **Delete permanently**, **Edit...**, the voice list, **Expand all** and **Collapse all**.

> Changing **Mode** or **Max words per caption** rebuilds the lines, but your fixes and hidden words are kept.
