# What Bones Caption Studio does

Bones Caption Studio turns the talking in your videos into stylish, word-by-word captions, like the ones on TikTok, Shorts and Reels.

1. You drop in a video.
2. It listens to the audio and writes down every word, with the exact time each word is said.
3. You pick how the captions look, fix any wrong words, and export.

![The main window: settings on the left, preview and transcript on the right](main-window.png)

Everything runs on your own PC. Your videos are never uploaded anywhere. The only time it uses the internet is to download a speech model the first time you use it, and when you click **Check for Updates**.

## What you can make

- **A complete video** (`.mp4`) with the captions burned in, ready to upload.
- **A vertical video** (1080×1920) for TikTok, Shorts and Reels, cropped from your wide video.
- **A caption overlay** (`.mov`, see-through) to drop on top of your video in an editor like DaVinci Resolve or Premiere.
- **Subtitle files** (`.srt` / `.vtt`) for YouTube or any video player.

## Made for gaming videos

- **Voices** gives each person their own caption colour, and lets you hide game characters with one click. See [Voices](topic:voices).
- **Audio tracks** lets you caption only your mic if you record it on its own track. See [Audio tracks](topic:audio-tracks).
