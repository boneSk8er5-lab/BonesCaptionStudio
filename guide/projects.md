# Save and open projects

A project (`.bcsproj` file) remembers the video, the transcript with all your fixes, the voices and the caption look, so you can come back later.

![The File menu](file-menu.png)

## Save

- **File** › **Save Project** (`Ctrl+S`) saves. The first time, it asks where.
- **File** › **Save Project As...** (`Ctrl+Shift+S`) saves a copy under a new name.

A `●` in the title bar means you have changes that aren't saved yet.

## Open

- **File** › **Open Project...** (`Ctrl+O`), or
- **File** › **Open Recent** for your last 10 projects, or
- drag a `.bcsproj` file onto the window, or
- double-click it in File Explorer (see below).

If the video has moved, the app asks you to find it.

## Start over

**File** › **New Project** (`Ctrl+N`) clears everything. If you have unsaved changes, it asks first.

## Autosave

Your work is saved in the background every 90 seconds. If the app or your PC ever closes unexpectedly, the app offers to **restore your unsaved work** the next time you open it.

## Double-click projects to open them

The installer sets this up for you. With the portable version, open **Help** and click **Open .bcsproj Files with Bones Caption Studio**.
