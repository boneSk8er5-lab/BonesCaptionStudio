# Settings and look

The app remembers everything you set: the model, the caption look, the window size and position, and your recent projects.

## Colour-blind friendly colours

Tick **Deuteranopia (Red-Green) Vision Assist** at the bottom of the left side. The teal accents change to a blue that's easier to tell apart.

## The Help menu

![The Help menu](help-menu.png)

- **User Guide** (`F1`): this guide.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts** (`Ctrl+F1`): every shortcut on one page.
- **Open Logs & Settings Folder**: where the log file and your settings are kept.
- **Open .bcsproj Files with Bones Caption Studio**: double-click projects to open them.
- **Check for Updates...**: tells you if there's a newer version on GitHub.
- **Reset Settings...**: puts every setting back to how it started. Your fonts, presets, voices and recent projects are kept.
- **About Bones Caption Studio**: version, edition and licences.

## Make the panels bigger or smaller

Drag the line between the left panel and the preview, or between the preview and the transcript. The app remembers where you leave them.

## Where things are kept

- **Installed version**: settings and logs in `%APPDATA%\BonesCaptionStudio`.
- **Portable version**: in the `data` folder next to `BonesCaptionStudio.exe`.
