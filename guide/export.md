# Export your video

Everything is in the **Export** panel. Click the **Target** button to choose what to make.

![The Export panel](export-panel.png)

## A complete video (.mp4)

1. Set **Target** to **Complete Video + Audio (.mp4)**.
2. Click **Render Full Video** and choose where to save it.

The captions are burned into the video, and the sound is kept.

## A vertical video for TikTok, Shorts and Reels

1. Set **Target** to **Complete Video + Audio (.mp4)**.
2. Tick **Vertical Format (1080x1920)**.
3. Move the **Crop** slider to choose which part of your wide video stays in the frame (keep your facecam or the action in view). The preview shows the crop.
4. Click **Render Full Video**.

## A see-through caption overlay (.mov)

1. Set **Target** to **Caption Overlay Only (.mov)**.
2. Click **Export Overlay**.

Put the `.mov` on a track above your video in DaVinci Resolve, Premiere or another editor. Overlay files are big (about 4–5 MB for every second at 1080p), and the app warns you before making a very large one.

## Subtitle files

Click **Export Subtitles (.srt / .vtt)**. Upload the `.srt` to YouTube, or keep it next to your video for any player.

## While it exports

The progress bar shows how far along it is. **Cancel Export** stops it. The app checks there's enough free disk space before it starts.

> The **File** menu has the same choices under **Export**, and `Ctrl+E` exports a complete video.
