# How captions are split

The **Mode** button in **Caption Styling** decides how the words are grouped on screen. Click it to switch.

- **Mode: Sentence** starts a new caption at the end of each sentence (`.` `!` `?`).
- **Mode: Auto (pauses)** does the same, and also starts a new caption when the speaker stops talking for a moment. This stops a caption from hanging on screen through a long silence.
- **Mode: Word** shows one word at a time.

## Max words per caption

**Max words per caption** is the most words on screen at once. Long sentences are split into smaller captions. 3 to 5 is typical for short videos.

## Pause before new caption

In **Auto** mode, **Pause before new caption** is how long nobody has to speak before the next words start a new caption. Lower it (like `0.3 s`) for fast talkers, raise it if captions split too often.

> The spoken word is always highlighted as it's said, in every mode.
