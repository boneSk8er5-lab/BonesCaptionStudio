# Make the captions look good

Everything here is in the **Font** and **Caption Styling** panels. The preview updates as you change things.

![The Caption Styling panel](styling-panel.png)

## Font

- Type in **Search fonts...** to find one of the fonts installed on your PC.
- Click **⭐ Favorite Font** to keep a font at the top of the list.

## Colours

- **Text**: the normal colour of the words.
- **Highlight**: the colour of the word being spoken.
- **Outline**: the colour of the outline around the letters.

## On/off buttons

- **Background**: a dark box behind the captions.
- **Outline**: an outline around each letter. Easy to read on any video.
- **Drop Shadow**: a soft shadow behind the text. Change its **strength** (up to 300%), **angle**, **distance** and **softness** in the rows below it.
- **Align**: put the captions at the **Bottom**, **Center** or **Top**.

## Size and position

- **Text size**: how big the captions are.
- **Caption height**: nudges the captions up (minus) or down (plus) from where **Align** puts them.

## Timing offset

If the captions feel a little early or late, change **Timing offset**. Minus shows them earlier, plus shows them later. It also applies to exports and subtitle files.

## Presets

Got a look you like? Click **Save** next to the preset box and give it a name. Pick it from the box any time to use it again. **Delete** removes the selected preset.
