# Voices: a colour for each person

When more than one person talks, give each voice its own colour so viewers can tell who is speaking. It's also the fastest way to hide game characters.

![The Voices panel](voices-panel.png)

## Find the voices automatically

1. In **Voices**, set **Number of voices** to how many people talk. **Auto** works too, but the exact number gives the best results.
2. Click **🎙 Detect Voices** (`F6`).
3. Each line gets a voice, and each voice gets its own row in the panel.

Tick **Detect voices after transcribing** to do this every time you click **Generate Captions**.

## Change a voice

- **Name**: click the name box and type, like `Vyper`.
- **Colour**: click the colour square.
- **Hide**: hides every line from that voice. Great for game characters or a TV in the background. Click **Show** to bring them back.
- **✕**: removes the voice. Its lines keep their words, just with no voice.

## Fix who said what

Detection isn't perfect, especially with game audio. Select the lines in the Transcript Editor, then:

- press `1` to `9` to give them that voice, or `0` for no voice, or
- click **Set Voice ▾** and pick one (or **+ New voice**).

## How the colours show

- **Voice colours: Highlight**: the spoken word uses the voice's colour.
- **Voice colours: Whole line**: the whole caption uses the voice's colour.
- **Voice colours: Off**: normal colours.

**Name tags: ON** puts the name before each caption, like `Vyper: let's go`. Leave it **OFF** for captions without names.
